SMS Policy Protocols

Learn the exact carrier laws and legal mechanisms I implement into your portfolio setup to avoid liability issues.

Essential Brand Audit Protocol

  • Express Written Consent: Never buy cold phone lead lists. Every contact on your phone file list must explicitly click to join.
  • Direct Disclosure Terms: Pop-up checkouts must include legal links to Privacy Policy and terms highlighting that message & data rates can apply.
  • CTIA Compliant Keywords: Carriers mandate keyword recognition. Sending STOP or END must block outgoing sequences in 0 seconds.
  • Carrier Registry (10DLC): Brands must register brand identity and campaign flow copy directly with cellular networks to ensure high delivery.

Compliance & Automation Answers

Common questions regarding legal requirements, 10DLC registrations, and integration procedures.

The Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) is a federal law regulating telemarketing. Violations carry massive penalties: $500 to $1,500 *per text message sent* without prior express written consent. My compliance setup structures checkbox consent legal terms and dynamic opt-out keywords to insulate your business from these penalties.

10-Digit Long Code (10DLC) is the system carrier networks (AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile) require for business texting. If your brand is not registered, carrier spam filters will block up to 80% of your messages. I handle your registry submission, campaign descriptions, and use-case approvals to ensure high delivery scores.
