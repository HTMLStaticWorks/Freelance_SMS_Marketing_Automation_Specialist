The Death of 'Co-Registration' Consent Sharing

For years, lead networks shared consumer phone lists via small pre-checked terms. Under the new FCC 'one-to-one' consent decree, businesses are forbidden from buying these bulk shared lists. Consumers must explicitly check a separate box for *each specific company* they wish to receive text updates from. Failure to implement this immediately results in automatic TCPA prosecution.
